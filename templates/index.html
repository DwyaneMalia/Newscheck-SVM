About this project

NewsCheck demonstrates supervised text classification on historical English-language news articles. It classifies text patterns associated with the dataset labels; it does not verify facts.

For important claims, check the publisher, date, author, cited sources, and other reliable reporting.

i

A text classifier, with limits.
